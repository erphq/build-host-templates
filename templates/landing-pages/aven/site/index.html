A few useful answers about the workspace, the plans, and this interactive concept.

Ask a question
Who is Aven designed for?

Aven is a concept for small and growing teams that want a clearer way to coordinate people, projects, and progress. The sample workspace shows how that experience could feel for a studio, product team, or independent business.

Can we start with a small team?

Yes. The Start plan is designed around one workspace and three team members. You can explore the larger plans as your needs grow; all pricing on this concept site is illustrative.

Does Aven replace our existing tools?

The idea is to bring useful context together, rather than make your team start over. The integration diagram demonstrates the kinds of connections a production service could support. No accounts are connected in this demo.

What does getting started look like?

Begin with one project, invite the people who need to be involved, and agree on a simple review rhythm. The sample setup checklist is designed to keep the first steps focused and manageable.

What happens when I send an inquiry here?

This is a working website concept. The inquiry form validates your details and saves a draft on this device, which you can review and copy. It does not send an email, create a booking, or subscribe you to anything.
