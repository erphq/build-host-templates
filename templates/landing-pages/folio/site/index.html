A few good
questions.

Is this a working product?

This is a complete interactive product demonstration. Projects, briefs, comments and approvals work locally in your browser. There is no backend account or external collaboration service.

Can I create my own project?

Yes. Complete the brief form to create a local project. It appears in the workspace and has its own overview, brief and milestones.

Will my feedback be sent to anyone?

No. Every comment and decision is stored locally. No email, invitation, client message or approval is transmitted.

Can I reset the demo?

Yes. The workspace includes a reset action that restores the original sample projects and clears local additions.
