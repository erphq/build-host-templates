A few things to know.

Is Night Shapes a real event?

No. This is an original festival-site concept with generated performer imagery and a fictional programme.

Does saving a set book anything?

No. My night is a browser-local schedule. Saving a set does not buy a ticket, reserve entry, or contact an artist.

How does calendar export work?

The download contains your selected fictional sets as calendar entries. Times follow the local clock in your calendar. No calendar is modified automatically.

Are the pass prices real?

No. The pass calculator uses illustrative prices only. There is no checkout or payment collection.
