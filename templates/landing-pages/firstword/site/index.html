SPACE TO THINK

The first words
aren’t the
final words.

A brief is an invitation to make something better. Firstword keeps the structure useful and the decisions open.

Nothing is invented for you. Your original notes stay intact, and every part of the result is editable.

A few things
worth knowing.

How does Firstword arrange my notes?

Your first non-empty line becomes the opening objective. All your notes stay as context. A project-specific list of deliverables and questions gives you a starting structure. This demo does not call an AI model.

Can I edit the entire brief?

Yes. Objective, context, audience, deliverables, questions, success criteria, and timing are all editable. Preview the exact Markdown before exporting.

Where does my writing go?

It stays in this browser. One current brief is saved locally when storage is available. Arranging another brief replaces it; download a copy when you want to keep one.

What if storage or copying is unavailable?

Your result stays available on the page with a plain-text copy field. You can select and copy it manually, or download Markdown from the editor.
