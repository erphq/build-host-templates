A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before we begin.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Sequence is a fictional studio exploring brand identity and digital experience design. The projects here demonstrate a connected approach across strategy, visual identity, websites, and product direction.

Do you work with an existing identity?

A strong starting point is something to build on. A project can focus on the website or a new part of a brand, with the existing identity providing the foundation.

What does the process look like?

We begin with context, define a direction, explore the system, and refine the experience. The studio page describes the four stages and the decisions each one resolves.

How can I start a brief?

Use the conversation page to describe your context and select a scope. It creates a local brief you can copy or download. Nothing is submitted or sent.
