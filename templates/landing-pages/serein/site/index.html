A little clarity
before you explore.

Is this connected to a real product?

No. The demo uses a fixed, original synthetic dataset. It does not collect visitor behavior or connect to an analytics provider.

What changes when I select a segment?

The chart, totals, comparison, funnel, and table all recalculate from that segment’s records. All visitors combines the two sample segments.

Where are my saved views?

In this browser’s local storage. If it is unavailable, the page explains that saving failed. Export a report to keep a portable copy.

Does a change prove why something happened?

No. This demo reports descriptive changes. It does not establish causation or statistical significance.
