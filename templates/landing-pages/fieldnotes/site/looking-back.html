Interview transcript

A fictional interview with an occasional contributor returning to a workspace after two weeks. The discussion explores memory, finding recent changes, and deciding which information still matters.

RResearcher
What is the first thing you look for when you return?

03Participant 0303:02 / Fictional response
I want to know what changed since I was last here. The project looks familiar, but that does not tell me whether the decision I remember is still the current one.

RResearcher
How do you find the information you need?

03Participant 0307:11 / Fictional response
I search for a phrase I remember from the conversation. I do not usually remember the document title, especially if someone renamed it later.

RResearcher
What makes you confident that you have the right context?

03Participant 0311:27 / Fictional response
A dated summary with links to the original discussion would help. I want the short version first, but I need a way to check the source if something feels unclear.
