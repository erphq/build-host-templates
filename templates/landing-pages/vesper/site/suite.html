Stay in. The city will wait.

The Afterhours Suite is less about spectacle than the luxury of a second room. A low partition separates sleeping and sitting, allowing one person to read while another rests. The city becomes a backdrop instead of a schedule.

For up to four guests using a king bed and double sofa bed. The sofa bed shares the sitting area, so privacy differs from a two-bedroom suite. The estimator treats this as one room with one nightly rate.

Who does this room suit?

For up to four guests using a king bed and double sofa bed. The sofa bed shares the sitting area, so privacy differs from a two-bedroom suite. The estimator treats this as one room with one nightly rate.

What should I ask about access?

This is a split-zone concept, not a verified accessible suite. Ask about thresholds, bathroom fittings, bed heights, and the route from the lift in your draft. No physical hotel or room inspection is implied.

How does the estimate work?

The estimator multiplies £320 by the number of nights. Optional breakfast adds £18 per guest per morning. These are fictional example amounts, not a quote, tax calculation, or payment request. The draft remains on this device.
