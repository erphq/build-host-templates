KNOW WHAT IS IN THE PACK

Boxes are packs,
not single pieces.

A quantity of two on Box of 4 morning buns means two boxes, eight buns, and twice the box price. The basket shows the selected pack and the number of pieces inside it.

Whole and sliced loaves are separate options. They stay separate in the basket even though this sample prices them equally.

THE DRAFT IS THE FINISH LINE

Keep the plan.
Nothing is placed.

Preparing a draft shows your selected bakes, pack quantities, subtotal, sample pickup day, and window. Download a Markdown copy if you want to keep it.

No bakery receives the draft. No stock is reserved, no card is charged, and no order number or pickup confirmation is issued.

RECIPE NOTES & LOCAL STORAGE

A few honest boundaries.

Recipe notes describe fictional product ideas and are not verified ingredient or allergen information. No health, dietary, or nutrition claims are made.

Your basket and chosen pickup plan can be saved in this browser. If storage is unavailable, the interface explains that limitation. Changing browsers or clearing site data can remove the local plan.
