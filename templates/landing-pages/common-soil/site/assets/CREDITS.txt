COMMON SOIL — ORIGINAL NONPROFIT CONCEPT

Common Soil is a fictional community-garden nonprofit website. Garden names, neighbourhoods, project proposals, stories and activities are original illustrative content. No real addresses, attendance, harvest, volunteer, financial, ecological or social-impact totals are claimed. No donations, registrations or messages are processed.

Original work: brand writing, project and editorial stories, interface code, neighbourhood.svg and neighbourhood.webp. The map is an imaginary schematic, not a navigation map or a record of real garden locations.
